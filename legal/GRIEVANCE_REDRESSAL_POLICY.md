## 3. Statutory Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

All grievances filed through our statutory channels are subject to strict legal response timelines:

| Stage | Statutory SLA | Action Taken |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Registration & Acknowledgment** | **Within 72 Hours** | Ticket issued (`GRV-YYYY-XXXXXX`), unique tracking link provided, complainant notified by email. |
| **2. Emergency Content Violations** | **Within 24 Hours** | Immediate triage for CSAM, non-consensual sexual depictions, or imminent threat of physical violence. |
| **3. Formal Resolution** | **Within 15 Days** | Detailed written investigation finding dispatched to complainant, corrective measures executed. |

---

## 4. Categories of Grievances Handled

1. **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP)**: Inquiries regarding consent withdrawal, personal data rectification, vector decoupling, or machine-readable exports.
2. **Child Safety & COPPA Compliance**: Inquiries regarding minor protections, stranger DM blocking, or parental verification.
3. **Defamation, Harassment & Impersonation**: Complaints regarding targeted abuse, impersonation accounts, or defamatory material.
4. **Intellectual Property Claims**: Copyright and trademark disputes outside automated DMCA channels.

---

## 5. Escalation & Appellate Mechanism

If you are not satisfied with the resolution provided by the Resident Grievance Officer, you may appeal the decision:
- **India**: Within 30 days of the resolution, to the **Grievance Appellate Committee (GAC)** established by the Central Government of India under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2022.
- **Global**: To the relevant sovereign Data Protection Authority (e.g. EU national supervisory authority under GDPR Art. 77).
