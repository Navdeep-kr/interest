# Interest Community Guidelines
**Last Updated: October 12, 2026 | Effective Date: October 12, 2026 | Version: 3.0**

---

## 1. Our Mission

**Interest** connects creators and viewers exclusively through authentic passion, skill, and curiosity. To preserve this sanctuary for craft and niche exploration, every creator and community member must uphold these Community Guidelines.

---

## 2. Zero-Tolerance Safety Violations (Immediate Ban & Law Enforcement Referral)

### 2.1 Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) & Minor Exploitation
We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse material or any form of minor exploitation. We hash all uploads against PhotoDNA/MD5 databases and immediately refer violations to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant sovereign law enforcement authorities.

### 2.2 Violent Extremism & Terrorism
Content promoting, praising, or supporting terrorist entities, violent mass casualty events, or extremist manifestos is strictly prohibited.

---

## 3. Safety & Dignity Standards

### 3.1 Harassment & Cyberbullying
Targeted harassment, doxxing, coordinated brigading, or non-consensual sharing of intimate images is banned. Creators have access to comment moderation tools, keyword blocklists, and strike options.

### 3.2 Hate Speech
Content attacking individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, religious affiliation, sexual orientation, disability, or gender identity is removed upon detection.

### 3.3 Explicit Nudity & Pornography
Sexually explicit videos, pornography, and non-consensual depictions are prohibited. Artistic nudity must be strictly age-restricted where permitted under cultural and legal norms.

---

## 4. Integrity & Synthetic Media (AI Transparency)

### 4.1 Mandatory AI-Generated Content Labeling
All reels generated, cloned, or heavily altered using synthetic generative AI models (visuals or audio) **MUST** be labeled with our platform tag (`isAiGenerated: true`, `aiLabelType: SYNTHETIC_MEDIA`). Failure to declare AI-generated media results in algorithmic downranking and warnings.

### 4.2 Bot Manipulation & Fake Engagement
Purchasing artificial views, bot-directed likes, automated comment farming, or coordinate spam runs triggers automated rate-limiting and creator fund disqualification.

---

## 5. Intellectual Property & Copyright

Respect original creators. Only upload content that you have created, licensed, or obtained valid authorization to distribute. Infringing audio or video is subject to automated fingerprint matching and 17 U.S.C. § 512 DMCA takedowns. Repeat infringement (3 strikes) leads to permanent creator account termination.

---

## 6. Enforcement Tiers

When violations occur, our moderation system applies escalating consequences:
1. **Content Removal**: Offending reel is deleted or archived.
2. **Strike 1 (Warning)**: Content removed, 24-hour publishing cooldown.
3. **Strike 2 (Temporary Suspension)**: 7-day publishing and monetization freeze.
4. **Strike 3 (Permanent Ban)**: Account termination and forfeiture of creator payouts.

All decisions may be appealed within 14 days via our in-app Creator Appeals Queue.
